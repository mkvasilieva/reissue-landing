Deleting your data

Last updated 29 October 2026

Delete everything Reissue holds about you

  1. Email hello@reissue.studio from the email address you sign in to Reissue with.
  2. Use the subject "Delete my Reissue data".
  3. We'll reply to confirm, then delete your account, notes, voice recordings, transcripts, ideas, drafts, library photos and videos, voice profile, and any LinkedIn or Instagram access within 30 days.

Remove Reissue from Instagram

  1. In Instagram, open your profile and tap the menu.
  2. Go to Settings, then Website permissions, then Apps and websites.
  3. Find Reissue under Active and tap Remove.

Reissue can no longer post to your account once it's removed. Removing it doesn't delete what we've stored; email us for that.

Remove Reissue from LinkedIn

  1. In LinkedIn, go to Settings and privacy, then Data privacy, then Permitted services.
  2. Find Reissue and tap Remove.

Questions: hello@reissue.studio. Full details are in our privacy policy.
