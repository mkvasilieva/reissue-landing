Reissue

Privacy policy

Last updated 29 October 2026

Reissue turns what you say into posts in your own voice. To do that we keep your notes, your writing and, if you connect them, access to post to your social accounts. This page says exactly what we collect, why, who helps us run it, and how to get it deleted.

The short version. Your notes and posts are yours. We use them only to run Reissue for you. We never sell them, never use them for ads, and never post anything without your tap. The AI services we use don't train their models on what you send through Reissue. Email hello@reissue.studio and we'll delete everything.

Who we are

Reissue is run by Marina Vasilieva in Sydney, Australia. Contact: hello@reissue.studio. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

What we collect

On reissue.studio (this website)

If you join the waitlist: your name, email and anything you choose to tell us in the form. The form is run by Tally. This site uses no tracking or advertising cookies.

In the Reissue app (app.reissue.studio)

  • Your account: your email address, used to sign you in with a code.
  • What you capture: voice notes (the audio and a written transcript), typed notes, links you paste, and photos, screenshots or documents you upload.
  • Your library: photos and videos you add, and a written description of each so Reissue can suggest one for a post.
  • Your writing: past posts you paste or upload as screenshots, so drafts sound like you, plus the voice profile Reissue writes from them.
  • What Reissue makes for you: ideas pulled from your notes, drafts, your edits and your feedback ("sounds like me", "that was off").
  • Your topics: the industry topics you choose, and what Reissue finds people discussing about them.
  • Connected accounts (only if you connect them):
    • LinkedIn: your LinkedIn member ID, your name, and an access token that lets Reissue publish a post to your profile when you tap Post.
    • Instagram: your Instagram professional account ID, username, and an access token that lets Reissue publish a post to your account when you tap Post.
    We don't read your feed, your messages, your followers or your past posts through these connections.

How we use it

  • To turn your notes into ideas and drafts in your voice, and to suggest which of your own photos or videos fits a post.
  • To tell you when something you've said lines up with what your industry is discussing.
  • To publish a post to LinkedIn or Instagram, only when you tap Post on that specific post.
  • To keep Reissue working, fix problems and improve how well drafts match your voice. During testing, Marina may read what you save to understand what's working; you're told this when you sign in.

We don't sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except the services below that help run Reissue.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open. Social access tokens expire on their own (LinkedIn after about 60 days) and are deleted when you disconnect or delete your account. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete your notes, audio, library, drafts, voice profile and tokens within 30 days, including from backups as they cycle.

Deleting your data

Email hello@reissue.studio from the address you sign in with, with the subject "Delete my Reissue data". We'll confirm and delete everything listed above within 30 days. You can also remove a single picture from your library in the app at any time.

To remove Reissue's access to your social accounts straight away: on LinkedIn, Settings, Data privacy, Permitted services; on Instagram, Settings, Website permissions, Apps and websites. Full steps: data deletion instructions.

Security

Your information is stored with row-level security, so each account can only ever read its own data. Social tokens are kept on the server and never sent to your phone or browser. Audio and pictures sit in private storage.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you. If you're unhappy with how we've handled it, email us first; you can also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll tell you in the app or by email before it applies.
